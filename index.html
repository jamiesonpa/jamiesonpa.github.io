About
Ph.D.

Molecular Biology & Biochemistry, Texas A&M (2025). Dissected plant–fungal pathogen receptor biology with a focus on signal transduction and metabolite-level readouts.

10+

Peer-reviewed publications in journals including New Phytologist, The Plant Cell, Trends in Plant Science, and Biochemical Journal.

My graduate training in molecular biology and biochemistry gave me a deep technical foundation in biological mechanisms and experimental rigor. I specialized in HPLC-based analytical workflows to quantify biochemical outputs, troubleshoot complex biological systems, and connect molecular mechanisms to measurable, production-relevant phenotypes.

After my Ph.D. work, I joined ARK Investment Management as a biotech Equity Research Analyst, where I evaluated the scientific credibility, translational feasibility, and commercial potential of emerging biotechnology companies. I integrated primary literature analysis with financial modeling to assess platform risk, competitive differentiation, and long-term value creation — collaborating closely with scientists, investors, and global partners along the way.

Today, I spend my time on independent research, writing, and one-on-one tutoring — helping students get the same clarity around chemistry and biochemistry that took me a decade in the lab to build.
